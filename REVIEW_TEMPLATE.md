# REVIEW_TEMPLATE.md — Jessie's Weekly Review Packet

## How this works

Every Friday, Accomplish compiles everything produced that week into a single packet. Jessie skims it and replies with approve/fix notes. Accomplish applies the fixes before Monday. Estimated time: **60-90 minutes per week**. No filming, no writing, no posting.
