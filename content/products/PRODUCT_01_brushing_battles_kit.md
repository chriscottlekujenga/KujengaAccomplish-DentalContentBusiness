# PRODUCT_01.md — Stop-the-Brushing-Battles Kit

> Flagship product. Target buyer: parents of ages 2–8 fighting nightly brushing resistance. Positioning rule: sell the OUTCOME ("end the battles"), not the contents (worksheets).

## Product summary

- **Name:** Stop-the-Brushing-Battles Kit
- **Price:** $24 (digital download bundle)
- **Channels:** Etsy primary; site upsell from lead magnet welcome sequence
- **The promise:** Everything a parent needs to turn brushing from a nightly fight into a 21-day habit — the exact charts, streak system, reward tickets, and word-for-word scripts hygienists teach.

## Contents (7 items)

1. **The 21-Day Brushing + Flossing Tracker** (the lead magnet's full version) — morning/night boxes, sticker-ready, milestone badges at day 7/14/21
2. **Age-by-Age Brushing Guide (1-page quick reference)** — the 0-3 / 3-6 / 6-8 / 8-10 stages from the site's pillar post, condensed for the fridge
3. **Morning & Night Routine Charts (2 designs)** — visual step-by-step (brush, floss, rinse) for non-readers, mirrored for kid height
4. **The Reward System** — printable "Smile Tickets" + rewards menu template (non-candy rewards: extra story, choose the song, weekend breakfast pick)
5. **The Parent Script Cards (4 scripts)** — the de-escalation lines hygienists actually teach: the "race the timer" script, the "you brush, I check" handoff script, the "special helper" script for resisters, the "we never skip nights" script
6. **The Brushing Battles Troubleshooter (1-page flowchart)** — "Kid screams?" → try X. "Kid rushes?" → try Y. Diagnosis-free, behavior-focused
7. **The Sugar Snacking Rules card** — the 20-minute acid rule + the 5 house rules, fridge-ready

## Production notes

- Design in Canva AI from the brand template family (mint/teal, rounded sans-serif)
- Print-at-home friendly: 8.5x11, low ink coverage, grayscale-safe
- "Reviewed by Jessie, RDH" seal on every page + footer with site URL
- Export: single combined PDF + a ZIP of individual pages (Etsy buyers like both)
