> Homeschool variant of PRODUCT_02. Homeschool parents (9%/yr growth segment) buy complete structured units and need MULTI-AGE content — one unit teachable to a 5-year-old and a 10-year-old at once.

## Product summary

- **Name:** Dental Health Unit Study — Multi-Age Homeschool Pack (K–5)
- **Price:** $14 (digital download) — premium over classroom packs (homeschool buyers pay for multi-age adaptation)
- **Channels:** Etsy primary (homeschool buyers are heavy Etsy users); TPT secondary
- **The promise:** One dental health unit your 5-year-old AND your 10-year-old can do together — multi-age lesson plans, solo-work options for each grade band, and a family-style capstone.

## Contents (11 items)

1. **Multi-Age Lesson Plan (5 days, family-style)** — one 30-min group lesson/day, with age-split "deeper dive" assignments: K–2 track and 3–5 track
2. **Family Read-Aloud Guide** — "talking points" script for parents (no teacher-training needed): what to say, what to ask, common kid questions with RDH-reviewed answers
3. **Sugar Detectives (dual level)** — K–2: sort cards; 3–5: read real labels, rank, graph results
4. **The Eggshell Experiment (family version)** — everyone does the observation; 3–5 students write the science-method sheet
5. **Brushing Chart Craft** — each kid builds their own take-home 7-day tracker
6. **Myth vs. Fact (16 cards)** — K–2 reads as a game; 3–5 students write their OWN myth-busting card (extension activity)
7. **Copywork Pages (2 levels)** — "Brush twice a day for two minutes" (K–2 tracing; 3–5 cursive)
8. **Tooth Anatomy (2 levels)** — K–2 coloring; 3–5 labeled diagram + vocabulary crossword
9. **Family Capstone: The Smile Interview** — kids interview a parent/grandparent about brushing "in the old days" — oral history meets oral health (the activity homeschool families share on social)
10. **Editable Co-op Letter** — for families who teach co-op classes: supply list, station plan for 8–20 kids
11. **Parent Answer Guide** — everything the parent needs, no dental background assumed
