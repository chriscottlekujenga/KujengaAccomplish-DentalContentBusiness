# PRODUCT_02.md — Dental Health Month Classroom Pack

> Teacher product. Target buyer: K–2 elementary teachers, school nurses, homeschool co-op leaders. February (National Children's Dental Health Month) is the demand spike — list by January at latest.

## Product summary

- **Name:** Dental Health Month Classroom Pack (K–2)
- **Price:** $12 (digital download)
- **Channels:** Teachers Pay Teachers primary; Etsy secondary during February
- **The promise:** A full week of no-prep dental health activities — worksheets, take-home letter, lesson plan, and crafts — so teachers can teach it tomorrow morning without building anything.

## Contents (10 items)

1. **Lesson Plan (5 days, ~30 min/day)** — standards-aligned health/science framing: brushing basics, sugar and teeth, dental visits, flossing, review + celebration day
2. **"Brushing Basics" Worksheet (2 versions: pre-K and K–2)** — trace-the-routine, circle-the-healthy-choice
3. **The 2-2-2 Rule Poster (11x17 + 8.5x11)** — classroom wall version
4. **Sugar Detectives Activity** — sort snacks into "sometimes" vs "everyday" foods; includes 24 food cards + sorting mat
5. **Brushing Chart Craft** — kids decorate their own take-home 7-day tracker (mirrors the site's lead magnet design)
6. **"Happy Tooth / Sad Tooth" Sorting Game** — the classic, refreshed: what keeps teeth happy vs. what makes them sad
7. **Tooth Anatomy Coloring Page (2 versions)** — simple (pre-K) and labeled (K–2)
8. **Take-Home Parent Letter** — editable template: reinforces the week's lessons, links to free at-home resources
9. **Certificate of Completion** — "Dental Health Champion" fill-in-name
10. **Teacher's Answer Guide + Talking Points** — common kid questions with RDH-reviewed answers ("why do we spit?" "what's fluoride?")
