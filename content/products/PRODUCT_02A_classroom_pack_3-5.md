## Contents (10 items)

1. **Lesson Plan (5 days, ~40 min/day)** — deeper science track: plaque biofilm, the acid cycle, nutrition labels, dental careers, advocacy day
2. **The Eggshell Experiment Guide** — the classic vinegar-vs-eggshell acid demo, written up as a real science-method sheet (hypothesis, observation, conclusion)
3. **Sugar Label Detectives** — students read real nutrition labels and rank drinks by sugar content (includes 12 label cards + recording sheet — the "one juice box = X sugar cubes" activity)
4. **Myth vs. Fact Sorting Cards (16 cards)** — "sugar-free soda is safe for teeth," "brushing harder cleans better," "only kids get cavities" — the myth-busting format upper-elementary students love
5. **Tooth Anatomy Diagram + Vocabulary Crossword** — enamel, dentin, pulp, gingiva, plaque, tartar
6. **Reading Passage: "What Is Plaque?"** (~350 words, grade 4 level) + comprehension questions
7. **The 2-2-2 Rule Poster (upper-elementary design)** — bolder, less cutesy
8. **Dental Careers One-Pager** — hygienist, dentist, assistant, lab tech: what they do, how you become one
9. **Editable Take-Home Parent Letter (3–5 version)** — links to the site's free resources
10. **Teacher's Answer Guide + RDH Talking Points** — the "hard questions" edition (fluoride debates handled neutrally, why the dentist says different things to different kids, etc.)
