# LEAD_MAGNET_BRIEF.md — The 21-Day Brushing + Flossing Habit Tracker

> Created Day 2 of the accelerated sprint. Design spec for the free printable lead magnet. This is the email-capture engine — every pillar post funnels to it.

## The product

A printable PDF tracker that turns brushing (and flossing) into a 21-day streak game for kids — the single most-referenced resource in the content plan. Free in exchange for email signup.

## Page structure (5 pages, 8.5x11 portrait)

1. **Cover** — "Brush With Me: The 21-Day Brushing Challenge" + RDH-reviewed badge + kid-at-sink illustration
2. **How it works** (parent-facing, half page) — the 2-2-2 rule in 4 sentences; sticker/stamp system explained; the "21 days makes it automatic" framing
3. **Week 1 tracker** — 7 morning/night box pairs, kid-friendly illustration border
4. **Week 2 tracker** — same + first milestone badge ("7-Day Streak!")
5. **Week 3 tracker + completion certificate** — "21-Day Brushing Champion" fill-in-name certificate

## Design specs

- **Palette:** soft mint/teal + warm neutrals (brand colors from BRAND_BRIEF.md)
- **Font:** rounded sans-serif (consistent with site headers)
- **Kid-appeal:** bathroom-mirror icons, big check boxes, space for stickers
- **Parent-appeal:** clean, uncluttered, printer-friendly (low ink coverage)
- **Trust:** "Reviewed by Jessie, RDH" seal bottom-right of every page
- **Print mode:** grayscale-safe (test print before release)

## Conversion path

1. Pillar posts → inline CTA ("Download it here — email required")
2. Pinterest Pin 10 → email capture page
3. Signup → instant delivery via MailerLite automation + welcome email #1
4. Welcome email #2 (day 2): "The 3 mistakes parents make with kids' brushing"
5. Welcome email #3 (day 5): introduce the Stop-the-Brushing-Battles Kit ($24 Etsy)
6. Weekly newsletter: one routine tip + one affiliate product spotlight

## Success metrics

- Signup conversion: target 3-5% of post visitors (industry norm for printables: 2-4%)
- Welcome-email open rate: target 45%+ (welcome emails outperform)
- Kit purchase from list within 30 days: target 2-4%
