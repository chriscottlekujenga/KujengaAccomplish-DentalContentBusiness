# PINS_BATCH_01.md — First 15 Pinterest Pin Concepts

> Created Day 2 of the accelerated sprint. Each pin: target post, hook text, overlay style, and call to action. Design in Canva AI at 1000x1500 (2:3). Board strategy: pin each to 2-3 relevant boards over the first week (Kids' Health, Parenting Tips, Dental Health, Homeschool Resources, Classroom Ideas).

## Format rules (from buyer research)
- Outcome-first hooks (sell the result, not the topic)
- "Hygienist-reviewed" trust seal on every pin
- Bold readable text over soft mint/teal backgrounds, warm photography
- Every pin links to a post (via link) — never a bare homepage pin
