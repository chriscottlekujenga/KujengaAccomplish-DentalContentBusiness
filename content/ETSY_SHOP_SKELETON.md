
**Digital delivery:**
```
Files are delivered instantly and automatically by Etsy — nothing is emailed manually, nothing can get lost in transit. If your files don't appear, check spam first, then message us.
```

**FAQ (Etsy FAQ section):**
```
Q: How do I access my files?
A: Purchases → your Downloads page. Files are also linked in your email receipt.

Q: Can I print at a print shop?
A: Yes — files are standard 8.5x11 (A4 variants included in most products). Home printer, library, or print shop all work.

Q: What if my child/kind of printer/laminator...
A: Message us — genuinely. We're a small shop that answers.

Q: Classroom/co-op use?
A: One purchase = one family or one teacher's classroom. Co-ops and multi-teacher schools: message for a license (discounted per seat).

Q: Do you do custom orders?
A: Sometimes! School colors, teacher names, clinic-branded versions — message us with what you need.

Q: Is this dental/medical advice?
A: No — these are educational tools. They support great habits; your own dentist handles your specific care. (That's the whole reason everything's reviewed by an RDH — accuracy in the education, never a diagnosis.)
```
