# Contact

## Say hello

Questions about a routine, a correction from a fellow dental pro, a product you think deserves a look, or a partnership idea: this is the place.

### Email

**hello@brushwithme.com**

We read everything. Jessie reviews clinical questions personally. Most replies go out within 2 to 3 business days.

### What we answer

- Questions about any post on this site, including the reasoning behind a routine step
- Product suggestions for the review pipeline
- Corrections from dental professionals (we credit you if you want, or keep it private, your choice)
- Media, partnership, and affiliate inquiries

### What we cannot answer

We do not provide dental advice, diagnosis, or treatment by email. If you have a question about something going on in your mouth or your kid's mouth, the right move is calling your dental office. We teach the routine; your dentist handles the treatment.

### Report a correction

If we got something wrong, we want to fix it. Email us with the word "Correction" in the subject line and the post title or URL. Clinical corrections go straight to Jessie for review, and updates are noted on the post.
