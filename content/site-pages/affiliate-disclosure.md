# Affiliate Disclosure

Effective date: [launch date]

## The short version

Some links on Brush With Me are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission. It costs you nothing extra. It helps keep this site running and reviewed by a real hygienist. We only recommend products Jessie would hand to her own family.

## What this means

When you see a link to a product on this site, it may be an affiliate link. That means if you make a purchase through that link, the merchant pays us a small commission. You pay exactly the same price you would pay otherwise.

## Who we work with

Merchants may include Amazon (we are an Amazon Associate, which means we earn from qualifying purchases), Etsy sellers of products featured in our guides, and other dental and family product merchants. We will name the merchant near the link wherever practical.

## How we choose products

Every product we mention faces the same test as every article: would a hygienist hand this to her own family? Jessie Tang, RDH, reviews product claims, not just marketing copy. If a product does not pass, it does not get a link, no matter the commission.

✓ Reviewed by Jessie, RDH

## What affiliate money does not buy

A commission never changes what we say. We do not accept payment for positive coverage. We do not write "prevents cavities" or make treatment claims because a merchant wants them. If we cannot recommend a product honestly, we do not recommend it at all.

## Your trust

The routine advice on this site is free either way. If you buy through our links, thank you: it keeps the lights on and the hygienist reviewing. If you buy elsewhere, the advice is still yours to use.
