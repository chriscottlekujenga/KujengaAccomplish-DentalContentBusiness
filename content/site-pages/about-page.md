# About Brush With Me

## Oral care routines reviewed by a Registered Dental Hygienist.

Brushing teeth with kids is rarely the sweet photo from the commercials. It is more often a negotiation at 8pm with a four year old who would rather do anything else. We built this site for exactly that moment.

Brush With Me exists to give parents clear, calm, hygienist reviewed guidance for the daily routine: how to brush, how much toothpaste, when to floss, and how to make those two minutes something you and your kid both stop dreading.

### What makes us different

**A real hygienist reviews everything.** Jessie Tang, RDH, has 25 years of clinical dental hygiene experience. Every article on this site goes through her review before it publishes. If it is on this page, a hygienist has read it and put her name behind it.

✓ Reviewed by Jessie, RDH

**We teach the routine; your dentist handles the treatment.** We are educators, not diagnosticians. We will never tell you what is wrong with your teeth or your kid's teeth. We teach habits that protect enamel and make the twice a day routine something families actually keep. If your dentist gave you different instructions, follow theirs first.

**No fear, no shame.** You will not find scare tactics here. We do not write "or you'll get cavities" and we do not make you feel bad about the year brushing fell apart. We give you the next small win, not a lecture.

**Practical and specific.** Exact minutes. Exact amounts by age. Exact words to say at the sink. If a tip is not actionable tonight, it does not make the page.

### Who this site is for

Parents of babies through big kids who want to get the daily routine right without wading through conflicting advice. If you have ever stood at the sink wondering whether the order of brushing and flossing matters, or how much paste goes on a toddler brush, you are our reader.
