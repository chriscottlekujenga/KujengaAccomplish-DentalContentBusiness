# Privacy Policy

Effective date: [launch date]

## The short version

We collect as little as we can. If you email us, we keep your email to reply. If you join the newsletter, we keep your email to send it. We do not sell your data, ever. The rest of this page is the same idea with more detail.

## Who we are

Brush With Me (brushwithme.com) is an oral care education site. Questions: hello@brushwithme.com.

## What we collect

**Email you give us directly.** When you contact us or subscribe to the newsletter, we get your email address and whatever you chose to write.

**Newsletter data.** Our email list runs on a third party email provider (MailerLite). When you subscribe, your email address and subscription status are stored there. You can unsubscribe from any email with one click, and you can ask us to delete your information at any time.

**Standard site logs.** Like nearly every website, our hosting may keep server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and troubleshooting.

**Cookies.** The site may use cookies for basic functionality and, if enabled, for analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

**Analytics.** If we run site analytics, we use tools that tell us which posts are read and how people find the site, in aggregate. We do not build individual profiles.

## Advertising and affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the merchant may set its own cookies and may pay us a small commission. Those merchants have their own privacy policies, and we recommend reading them. Merchants we work with include Amazon and Etsy sellers. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

## What we never do

- We never sell your personal information.
- We never share your email address with advertisers.
- We never send medical or dental advice by email.

## Data retention

We keep contact emails as long as needed to handle your message. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Server logs are kept by our host for a limited period.

## Your choices

You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link at the bottom of every email. You can ask us to access or delete your data by emailing hello@brushwithme.com. You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time.

## Children's privacy

Our content is written for parents, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be noted on the site.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@brushwithme.com.
