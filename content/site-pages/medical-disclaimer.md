# Medical Disclaimer

## The short version

Brush With Me is an education site, not a dental office. Nothing here is dental or medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. If your dentist gave you different instructions, follow theirs first. The rest of this page is the same idea more formally.

## Education, not advice

All content on brushwithme.com, including articles, routines, emails, and product notes, is for general educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a licensed dentist or other qualified health professional.

Nothing on this site establishes a dentist patient or hygienist patient relationship. Reading a post does not make Jessie your hygienist and does not make Brush With Me your dental office.

## Follow your dentist first

Every routine we teach is general education for typically developing, healthy mouths. Your own dental professionals know your history and your kid's history. If your dentist or hygienist gave you instructions that differ from anything on this site, follow theirs first, always.

We teach the routine; your dentist handles the treatment.

## If something is wrong

If you or your child has pain, bleeding that does not stop, swelling, trauma to a tooth, or anything else that worries you: do not search for it, call your dental office. If it is an emergency, call emergency services.

## Age and situation specific care

Guidance on this site is organized by age, but every child is different. Routines for babies, toddlers, and kids assume healthy mouths and typical development. Special health conditions, medications, and developmental differences change what is right, and only your own dental team can tell you what applies.

## No guarantees

We teach habits that build a lifetime of easier brushing. We do not promise any outcome, and we never claim anything on this site prevents, treats, or cures any condition.

## Review standard

Every article is clinically reviewed by Jessie Tang, RDH, with 25 years of clinical dental hygiene experience. Clinical review makes content more trustworthy, not more medical. It remains education for the general public.
