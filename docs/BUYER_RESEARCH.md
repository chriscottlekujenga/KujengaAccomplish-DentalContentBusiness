# Buyer Research — Dental Content Business

## Core insight (correction from planning)

Parents do **not** buy generic kids' printables. Generic worksheets are free everywhere. Parents buy **outcomes** — e.g., a kit that "stops the brushing battles." The listing must sell the result to the adult, not the worksheet to the child.

## Buyer segments

### 1. Homeschool parents — PRIMARY
- Homeschooling grew ~9% last year; record enrollment in over a third of US states
- Habitual curriculum buyers; buy complete structured bundles, not single sheets
- Buy outcome-bundled kits ($19-29)
- Best served by: multi-age routine kits, printable brushing charts, habit trackers, parent scripts

### 2. Elementary teachers / school nurses — SEASONAL SPIKE
- 94-97% of teachers spend own money on classroom materials (~$3.24B/year combined)
- February = National Children's Dental Health Month = guaranteed demand spike
- Buy no-prep classroom packs ($8-15), take-home letters, lesson plans
- Best served by: grade-banded packs (K-2, 3-5), 30-student sets

### 3. Pinterest-scrolling moms (25-44)
- Etsy/Pinterest buyer base: women 25-44, mobile-first
- Trust RDH credential when buying oral care products
- Buy via affiliate links: kids' electric brushes, water flossers, toddler toothpaste
- Best served by: routine explainers with product recommendations

### 4. Routine-optimizer adults
- Just got "you need to floss better" lecture
- Subscribe for free printable, buy $29 routine protocol + affiliate upgrades
- Best served by: adult routine guides, brush/floss order explainers

### 5. Institutions (optional B2B)
- Preschools, pediatric dental offices
- Buy license packs or co-branded waiting-room education kits
- Higher value per deal but slower to land

## Product positioning rules

1. **Sell outcomes, not worksheets.** "Stop the Brushing Battles Kit" beats "Brushing Chart."
2. **Bundle everything needed.** Parent gets chart + tracker + reward system + script.
3. **Use the RDH credential everywhere.** "Created and reviewed by a Registered Dental Hygienist" is the differentiator.
4. **Avoid free-content territory.** Don't compete with what teachers already give away; compete with the result they can't get for free.
