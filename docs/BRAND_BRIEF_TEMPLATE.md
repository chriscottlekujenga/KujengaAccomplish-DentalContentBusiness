## 3. Positioning Statement

For **parents and teachers** who need **clinically trustworthy oral care guidance**, **[Brand]** is the **RDH-reviewed resource** that **turns confusing dental advice into simple routines and printable tools**. Unlike generic health blogs, every article and product is **reviewed by a Registered Dental Hygienist**.

## 4. Tone of Voice

- Warm, not clinical
- Practical and specific
- Never fear-based or shaming
- Always educational, never diagnostic
- "We teach the routine; your dentist handles the treatment"

## 5. Visual Direction

- Colors: soft teals, mints, warm neutrals, clean white space
- Imagery: diverse families, kids brushing, close-up smiles, clean flat-lay product shots (AI-generated)
- Fonts: rounded sans-serif for headers; clean readable serif or sans for body
- No medical diagrams that imply diagnosis

## 6. Core Promise

"Everything here is reviewed by a real dental hygienist, so you can teach your family better oral habits without wading through generic advice."

## 7. What We Will Never Say

- "Prevents cavities" on products (implied treatment claim)
- "Cure bad breath" (overpromise)
- "This replaces your dentist" (liability)
- Diagnostic language for specific conditions

## 8. First 5 Content Pillars

1. Kids' brushing routines by age
2. Correct order: brush, floss, mouthwash
3. Teacher/parent dental health month resources
4. Electric vs. manual brush for families
5. Common mistakes hygienists see
