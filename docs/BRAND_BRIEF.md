## 2. Recommendation

**Primary: Hygienist Reviewed — hygienistreviewed.com ($10-12/yr)**

Why it wins:
1. **The moat is the name.** The entire competitive edge is "a real RDH reviews everything." This name makes that unmissable in search results, pins, and Etsy listings.
2. **SEO compounding.** Queries like "are electric toothbrushes worth it hygienist" and "reviewed by dental hygienist" map directly onto the domain.
3. **Brand-flexible.** Works as an umbrella for the kids' line ("Hygienist Reviewed Kids"), teacher packs, and adult routines — nothing about the name locks us into one buyer segment.
4. **Ad copy writes itself.** "Every guide reviewed by a registered dental hygienist." Headline done.

**Runner-up: Smile Habits (smilehabits.com)** — if the owner prefers a warmer, less clinical feel for the Pinterest/mom audience, this is the pick. Could also be acquired later as the email/newsletter brand.

**Strategic option:** register hygienistreviewed.com as the site + smilehabits.com as a forward ($10 more) to hold both while the brand grows.

## 3. Positioning statement

For **parents and teachers** who need **clinically trustworthy oral care guidance**, **Hygienist Reviewed** is the **RDH-verified education resource** that **turns confusing dental advice into simple routines and printable tools**. Unlike generic health blogs, every article and product is **reviewed by a Registered Dental Hygienist (Jessie, RDH)**.

## 4. Tone of voice

- Warm, not clinical. Confident, not preachy.
- Practical and specific (exact minutes, exact amounts, exact ages).
- Never fear-based or shaming ("build the habit" beats "or you'll get cavities").
- Always educational, never diagnostic.
- Recurring line: "We teach the routine; your dentist handles the treatment."

## 5. Visual direction

- Colors: soft teals/mints + warm neutrals, generous white space.
- Imagery: diverse families, kids brushing, close-up smiles, clean flat-lay product shots (AI-generated).
- Fonts: rounded sans-serif headers; clean readable body.
- Badge: a recurring "✓ Reviewed by Jessie, RDH" checkmark seal on every post and product.

## 6. What we will never say

- "Prevents cavities" on products (treatment claim) — use "builds habits that protect enamel."
- "Cure bad breath" — use "helps manage bad breath."
- Anything that replaces a dentist: "If your dentist gave you different instructions, follow theirs first."
- Diagnosis of specific conditions.

## 8. Reviewer-locked decisions (2026-10-03, from Jessie's full review, submitted 2026-09-30)

> Jessie Tang completed the Packet 02 full review (review_2026-09-30_195211.txt via the site form). Result: 32 Approve / 5 Fix. These decisions are now permanent writing rules.

- **Byline (locks section 5 badge + all content):** "Jessie Tang, RDH, 25 years of clinical dental hygiene experience." Applied across all 15 posts, products, emails, pins, video CTAs.
- **Tone rule (from 10b):** No clinical jargon aimed at lay readers ("surviving proper hygiene" style phrasing banned). Write conversationally, like talking to the target market.
- **Punctuation rule (from 10b):** No em dashes or en dashes in any content, now and moving forward. Use commas, colons, or full stops. Applied: all 27 content files swept clean; verify every future draft.
- **First dental visit timing (11b):** "When the first tooth erupts" (post 11 wording). Note: posts 02, 03, 13 use "first birthday or within 6 months of first tooth," which follows ADA/AAPD guidance and was explicitly approved by Jessie in packet sections 13a-b and posts 01-08, so it stands. Post 11 uses eruption wording; both may appear across the site.
- **Electric brush claim rule (12a + 12b):** Never write "oscillating has more evidence" or "hygienists recommend electric" as the core claim. The rule: the person and their technique at the gumline matter more than the brush type. Electric brushes help by making good technique automatic (timer, pressure sensor). Manual users who miss the gumline get gumline cavities too. 12a approved with comment: "Depends on the person more than the brush."
- **Expertise boundary (12c):** Jessie has no expertise on UV sanitizers and whitening modes; that portion was removed from post 12 entirely. Rule: any feature claim outside hygiene technique and plaque removal goes back to Jessie before publication, or gets cut.
