# Investment Estimate — Dental Content Business

## Bottom line

| Scenario | Total capital needed (through breakeven) | Timeline to $1K net |
|----------|------------------------------------------|---------------------|
| Lean (organic only, no ads) | ~$600-800 | Month 5-6 |
| Standard | ~$1,000-1,300 | Month 5-6 |
| **Accelerated (recommended)** | **~$800-1,000** | **Month 4** |
| Slow ramp (+2 months) | ~$1,500-1,800 | Month 7-8 |

## One-time startup costs

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Domain (1st year) | $0 (free with Bluehost) |
| Hosting — Bluehost mid-tier, 12 months | ~$84 ($6.99/mo intro) |
| Business email | $0-6/mo |
| Etsy listing fees (first 40 products) | ~$8 |
| TPT seller account | $0 (free tier) |
| Pinterest Business account | $0 |
| Canva AI | $0 trial → $13/mo |
| AI image credits | $10-20 |
| Legal name filing (varies) | $0-50 |
| **Baseline startup** | **~$110-180** |

## Accelerated sprint extras

| Extra item | Cost | Purpose |
|------------|------|---------|
| Bulk AI content tool | $20/mo | 20-30 posts in 2 weeks |
| Fast-rank SEO tool | $50/mo | Quick indexing + tracking |
| Pinterest promoted pins | $3/day × 20 days = $60 | Early traffic momentum |
| Etsy ad test | $3/day × 20 days = $70 | Conversion data |
| Extra design credits | $15 | Mockups and pins |
| **Sprint extras** | **~$215** |

## Monthly operating costs

| Phase | Monthly run-rate |
|-------|------------------|
| Months 1-2 (building) | ~$40-60 |
| Months 3-4 (scaling) | ~$75-100 |
| Month 5+ (maturity) | ~$100-130 |

## Where every dollar goes (6 months, accelerated)

| Category | Amount |
|----------|--------|
| Hosting + domain (12 mo) | ~$95 |
| Email platform (free → paid tiers) | ~$45 |
| Canva AI + AI credits | ~$150 |
| Etsy fees + ads | ~$300 |
| TPT upgrade (optional, month 3+) | ~$60 |
| SEO tool + bulk content tool | ~$70 |
| Misc/contingency | ~$100 |
| **Total 6-month investment** | **~$820-1,200** |

## Biggest levers and cut-list

- **Biggest lever:** Etsy Ads spend. Cut it and save ~$300, but expect +2 months to target.
- **Do not cut:** RDH weekly review (license/product integrity) and daily Accomplish invocation (the engine).
- **Watch out for:** Bluehost renews at $9.99-13.99/mo after year 1. Budget or migrate in month 11.

## Break-even

After hitting consistent $1,000/month net, the total investment pays back in roughly **2 months** (month 7-8 of the business).
