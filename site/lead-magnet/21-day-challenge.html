How the challenge works

Three rules. That's the whole system.

1. Brush 2× a dayTwo full minutes each time — morning and night. Two minutes is longer than it feels; play a song and brush until it ends.
2. Check the boxes togetherMorning ☐ and night ☐, checked by the kid — ownership lives at eye level. Tape the chart at kid height.
3. Celebrate streaksBadges land at Day 7, 14, and 21. Missed a night? The streak resets — the world doesn't. Model the reset calmly.

Parent tips (from 25 years of chairside practice)

  • Stickers > candy. Let your kid pick the sticker sheet — the choosing is half the motivation.
  • Kid brushes first, parent "checks corners" after. Especially the back molars and the gumline.
  • Anchor the habit: brush right after breakfast and right before the bedtime story. Same order, every night.
  • Under 3? You do the brushing with a rice-grain smear of fluoride toothpaste; they get a "practice turn" after.
  • Never miss two in a row. One miss is a miss; two is the start of a new habit.
The 2-minute rule: two minutes divided by four corners of the mouth = 30 seconds each. A two-minute song or a brush with a built-in timer does the counting for you — for everyone.
